# Module 15: invoicing of plan and managed-service fees

Module 9 priced everything (a plan's monthly fee, its fees per AC and DC connector, its revenue share, the add-on prices) and left a note: _invoices, payment of the platform fees, dunning: the invoicing module_. Module 10 put the managed service in place and deferred _billing the managed service fee_ to the same place. This module builds it. On the 1st of each month the worker **drafts last month's invoice** for every tenant with something to bill: the plan fee, the connector fees for the connectors it has, the add-ons it holds, the managed service fee where there is an agreement, and the revenue share of the charging revenue of the month, each prorated to the days it applied, plus the tenant's tax rate. Wattwisely's billing staff **check the draft in the admin console**, adjust it, and **issue** it: it gets its number, falls due in 30 days and goes to the tenant's billing contact by e-mail. The tenant **reads its invoices in the portal** under Settings > Invoices and prints them. Staff **mark an invoice paid** when the money arrives, or **void** it; an invoice that is overdue marks the tenant's subscription past due until it is settled.

Stories covered: on 1 October the worker drafts Acme Charging's September invoice: Growth plan 299.00, two AC connectors at 10.00 and one DC at 16.00, the Load management add-on at 3.00 per connector, the managed service at 250.00 and 20 % tax on the lot, 712.80 in all; Ada opens Invoices in the console, sees the draft, adds a 50.00 credit for an outage with a reason, and issues it; Acme's accounts address gets the e-mail "Invoice WW-2026-0001 from Wattwisely: USD 662.80, due by 31 October", and Olivia opens the invoice under Settings > Invoices and prints it; Flex Ops, on the Flex plan with no monthly fee, is billed 5 % of its 200.00 of charging revenue; Volt Hotels, still on trial, is billed nothing and gets no draft; Acme's October draft is wrong because the plan changed mid-month, so Ada works the fee lines out again from today's plan and the change shows; a tenant that moved onto a plan in the middle of a month pays for the days it was on it; Acme does not pay, and on the 31st day its subscription shows "Past due" in the console and the portal's Plan page, until Ada marks the invoice paid with the bank reference; Vic, a viewer, sees every invoice and can change none; Fred, a Technician at Acme, cannot see the invoices at all; the audit log of Acme shows who drafted, changed, issued, settled or voided which invoice.

## Design decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A month is a calendar month in UTC, billed in arrears.** The first of the month is when last month is known in full: the connectors it had, the revenue it made. Billing in advance would mean credits for every change; in arrears the invoice states facts. Sessions, which drive the revenue share, are already stored in UTC                                                                                                |
| **Fees are prorated by covered days.** A plan that started on the 12th of a 30-day month is billed 19/30 of its fee; an add-on taken on the 20th, 11/30; a managed service accepted on the 1st, in full. A day counts when any part of it is covered. Days are what the contract talks about and what staff can check by eye; the line says `, 12–30 September (19 of 30 days)` so nobody has to work it out                      |
| **Connectors are counted when the invoice is drafted, not averaged.** The platform keeps no history of connector counts per day, and the fee is small; counting on the 1st is what module 9 said the fee is ("per connector in use"). A tenant that removes a charger on the 30th is not billed for it. Staff can work the lines out again later if the count was wrong                                                           |
| **The worker drafts, people issue.** A draft is not an invoice: it has no number, the tenant does not see it, and staff can change it. Issuing is one deliberate click, because a number cannot be taken back and the e-mail cannot be unsent. Later, when the drafts have been right for a few months, a setting could issue them without a click; the code path is the same                                                     |
| **Numbers come from `invoice_sequences`, per year, in the issuing transaction.** `WW-2026-0001`, no gaps, no reuse: the row for the year is locked by the upsert until the commit. Two staff issuing at once get consecutive numbers. A voided invoice keeps its number, as an accountant expects                                                                                                                                 |
| **Only drafts and issued invoices can be voided; nothing is deleted.** A paid invoice is a fact; a correction is a credit line on the next invoice. Voiding keeps the row with the reason and who did it, and frees the month: the partial unique index on `(tenant_id, period_start) where status <> 'void'` lets the month be drafted again                                                                                     |
| **Issuing refreshes the billing contact and nothing else.** The contact and the reference are copied from the subscription onto the invoice when it is issued, so the invoice records whom it went to even if the contact changes later. The amounts are not recalculated: what staff checked is what goes out. "Work the fee lines out again" is the explicit way to bring a draft up to date, and it keeps the adjustment lines |
| **Tax is one rate per tenant, on the whole invoice.** `tax_rate_bps` on the subscription (2000 is 20 %), set by staff, applied to the subtotal and rounded once. Per-line rates, reverse charge and tax registration numbers wait for an accountant's brief; the reference field already carries a VAT number when the tenant gives one                                                                                           |
| **The revenue share is billed in the plan's currency only.** Charging revenue in another currency is not converted: there is no rate to convert it at. The draft carries a remark naming the amount left out, so staff can add a line by hand or leave it                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Overdue means the subscription is past due; nothing is switched off.** Module 9 gave `past_due` a meaning (a notice in the portal, visible to staff); this module sets and clears it automatically from the invoices. Dunning e-mails and suspension are decisions for Wattwisely's finance process, not for a job to take on its own; staff suspend a tenant by hand as before                                                 |
| **The invoice e-mail is Wattwisely's, not the tenant's.** It uses the platform's own name and colours (`TenantBrand.defaults`), because the sender is Wattwisely and the reader is the tenant's accounts department. It goes to the billing e-mail only; no address, no e-mail, and the console says so                                                                                                                           |
| **Every change to an invoice is a versioned write with an audit entry in the tenant's log.** `If-Match` as everywhere since module 2. The entries (`admin.invoice_drafted`, `admin.invoice_changed`, `admin.invoice_issued`, `admin.invoice_paid`, `admin.invoice_voided`) land in the tenant's audit log so an Owner can see who did what to their invoice, as with every other action by staff                                  |
| **`packages/billing` holds the calculation, the worker and the API call it.** Both draft invoices (the worker on schedule, the API on a staff member's click) and both must agree to the cent. The calculator is pure and tested by itself; the loader and the writer around it take a transaction, so each caller runs them inside its own tenant and row-level security context                                                 |

## Running it

Apply the migrations as always (`npm run db:migrate` or `./scripts/up.sh`, which runs them). The worker has one new setting, `INVOICE_INTERVAL_MS` (an hour by default): how often it looks for months to draft and invoices gone overdue. `seed-demo.sh` puts both demo tenants on their plans three months back, gives Acme Charging 20 % tax and Volt Hotels' managed service a fee of 250.00, so there is a full month to bill:

1. Start everything and wait for the worker's first run (or set `INVOICE_INTERVAL_MS=10000` for the demo). Log in to the console as `admin@wattwisely.test` and open **Invoices**: two drafts for last month, one per tenant.
2. Open Acme Charging's draft: the Growth plan, its connectors, the Load management add-on, 20 % tax. Add a line, a credit of -50.00 with a reason, and watch the totals follow. Issue and send: the invoice gets its number and Mailpit (`mail.<host>`) shows the e-mail to `accounts@acme.test`.
3. Log in to the portal as `owner@acme.test` and open **Settings > Invoices**: the invoice is there, open; print it or save it as PDF from the browser.
4. Back in the console, mark it paid with a reference. Try to void it: the API refuses, a paid invoice is final.
5. On Volt Hotels' draft, note the managed service line, then void the draft with a reason, open the tenant and **Draft an invoice** for the same month again.
6. To see the overdue path without waiting a month, set an issued invoice's `due_at` in the database to yesterday; within the hour the tenant's plan shows **Past due** in the console and the portal, and the Plan page says payment is overdue. Mark the invoice paid and it clears on the next run.
